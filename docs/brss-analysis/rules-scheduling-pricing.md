# BRSS business rules: scheduling, unavailability, trucks/warehouses, pricing, zip zones, dashboard, order wizard

Source: `sai-cto/brss-salesforce` at commit `233cf40` (read-only). Every citation is `path/from/repo/root:line`.
Rule format: `R-SCH-nnn | rule | citation(s)`. Open questions are `Q-SCH-nn` in section 11.
Rules describe what the code does, not what the business may intend. Where the two might differ, the rule points at an open question.

Three findings that change the shape of the rewrite, stated up front:

- **There is no staff orders dashboard in the code.** `BRSS_OrdersDashboard` is the *customer's* "Order & Appointment Management" page, hard-filtered to the logged-in user, with one toggle. Staff work from standard Salesforce list views (section 3).
- **Unavailability and lead-time rules are enforced only in the browser date picker.** Nothing on the server rejects a blocked date, a blocked time slot, or a double booking (sections 1.4 and 2).
- **Prices are not quoted at booking.** The wizard only authorises a card; storage and shipping line items are generated by the crew at pickup from measured weight (sections 1.6 and 5).
