# Hired Hands test site: how to try it

Thank you for testing! This is the new system that would replace Movegistics. Everything on the
test site is **fake**: fake customers, fake card payments, and no email is really sent. You can't
break anything, so click around freely.

## Sign in

1. Open the link you were sent. Type the **site password** (the same for everyone).
2. Sign in with **your own email and password** (sent to you separately).
3. In the left menu, open **Hired Hands Moving Company**, then the **App** tab.

The app has five sections: **Dashboard, Leads, Jobs, Schedule, Invoices** (plus **Settings**).
Each move lives on **one Job page**: its price, estimate, booking, days, invoice, payments,
calls and notes.

## Five things to try (about 20 minutes)

1. **A new lead.** Leads → **Add lead**. Fill in a name, an email (any made-up address, e.g.
   `test1@example.com`), a source and a move date. Then **Convert to job**.
2. **Price it.** On the Job page, in **Quote**, pick an item (for example "2 Men"), enter the
   hours and click **Add line**. The totals update as you type. The 16' truck is already there.
3. **Send the estimate.** Click **Send estimate**. Then click **Copy link** and open that link
   in a **private / incognito window**: you are now the customer. Go through the 4 steps,
   sign, and **Pay deposit** with the test card.
4. **Book it.** Back on the Job page (reload it) you'll see **Ready to book**. Click **Book this
   move** and check it on the **Schedule** (Day / Week / Month).
5. **Invoice and get paid.** On the Job page enter the actual hours worked and click
   **Create invoice**, then **Send invoice**. Use **Copy payment link** to pay as the customer,
   or **Receive a payment** for a check. The job moves to **Closed**.

Also worth a look: a lead you couldn't reach (**Couldn't reach** sends 3 follow-up emails),
**Log a call** on a job, and **Settings** (prices, discounts, email wording).

## Good to know

- **Placeholder text:** the terms and the email wording are marked "PLACEHOLDER – manager to
  replace". We'd love your real wording.
- **The 4%:** the test site adds a 4% fee **only to card payments**, and no service tax. Tell
  us which HHMC actually charges.
- **Emails:** these are listed under the **Data** tab → Notifications, not sent.
