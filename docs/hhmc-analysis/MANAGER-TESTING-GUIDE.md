# Hired Hands pilot: how to use it

Thank you for trying the new system that will replace Movegistics. This is a **pilot**: you can
enter real moves, but two things are switched off on purpose until we're ready:

- **No email reaches a customer.** Use **Copy link** and send the link yourself (text or your
  own email).
- **Cards can't be charged.** Record **cash or check** payments. The customer's page says
  "We'll arrange payment with you directly".

A blue **PILOT** bar at the top of every page reminds you.

## Sign in

1. Open the link you were sent and click **Sign in with Microsoft 365**.
2. Use your **SAI Microsoft account** (the one you use for Outlook and Teams). There's no
   separate password.
3. In the left menu, open **Hired Hands Moving Company**, then the **App** tab.

If it says it can't sign you in, you haven't been given access yet: ask [your name / contact].

## Practice first: Test mode

The switch at the top right says **Live** or **Test mode**.

- **Test mode** is full of fake practice moves. Nothing there is real, and the test card works,
  so you can try every step, including paying by card.
- **Live** is for HHMC's real jobs. It starts empty.

The app has five sections: **Dashboard, Leads, Jobs, Schedule, Invoices** (plus **Settings**).
Each move lives on **one Job page**: its price, estimate, booking, days, invoice, payments,
calls and notes.

## Five things to try in Test mode (about 20 minutes)

1. **A new lead.** Leads → **Add lead**. Fill in a name, an email (a made-up address such as
   `test1@example.com`), a source and a move date. Then **Convert to job**.
2. **Price it.** On the Job page, in **Quote**, pick an item (for example "2 Men"), enter the
   hours and click **Add line**. The totals update as you type. The 16' truck is already there.
3. **Send the estimate.** Click **Send estimate**, then **Copy link**, and open that link in a
   **private / incognito window**: you are now the customer. Go through the 4 steps, sign, and
   **Pay deposit** with the test card.
4. **Book it.** Back on the Job page (reload it) you'll see **Ready to book**. Click **Book this
   move** and check it on the **Schedule** (Day / Week / Month).
5. **Invoice and get paid.** Enter the actual hours worked and click **Create invoice**, then
   **Send invoice**. Use **Copy payment link** to pay as the customer, or **Receive a payment**
   for a check. The job moves to **Closed**.

## Then a real move (Live)

Switch to **Live** and do the same steps with a real customer. The differences are:

- send the estimate link to the customer yourself;
- take the deposit and the balance as **cash or check** with **Receive a payment**.

Please keep Movegistics running alongside for now: this pilot doesn't replace it yet.

## Good to know

- **Placeholder text:** the terms and email wording are marked "PLACEHOLDER – manager to
  replace". Please send us HHMC's real wording.
- **The 4%:** the system adds a 4% fee **only to card payments**, and no service tax. Tell us
  which HHMC actually charges.
- **Emails** the system would have sent are listed under the **Data** tab → Notifications.
- **Adding a colleague** (general manager): **People and access** → **Add person**, with their
  SAI Microsoft email.
