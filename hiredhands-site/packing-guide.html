Packing Guide

Pack smart. Move fast. Arrive intact.

General packing tips

  • Pack one room at a time and label every carton with its contents and destination room.
  • Start with items you use least (seasonal clothing, décor, books) well ahead of moving day.
  • Keep packed boxes as close to the door as possible for efficient loading.
  • Heavy items go on the bottom, lighter items on top, in every box, every time.
  • The heavier the item, the smaller the box. Large boxes are for light, bulky things only.
  • Secure every carton bottom with tape before filling. A loaded box with a weak bottom is a disaster waiting to happen.
  • Have the right materials on hand before you start: tape, bubble wrap, markers, and the right box sizes.

Bureaus & dressers

Do

  • Remove anything that could break. Partially empty drawers and fill gaps with small wrapped fragile items like clocks or picture frames.

Don’t

  • Leave drawers completely full. The bureau becomes too heavy to move safely.
  • Overload drawers. Damage to the piece or its contents is likely.
  • Tape drawers shut directly. Tape can pull finish off wood surfaces.

Delicates: china & crystal

Do

  • Use small or medium boxes only. Never use large ones for delicates.
  • Wrap every glass and cup individually in bubble wrap or unprinted newspaper.
  • Seal and clearly mark every box containing fragile items.
  • Line the box bottom with padding before placing anything inside.

Don’t

  • Use compartmentalized liquor boxes unless the compartments are sturdy cardboard with a padded base.
  • Forget to mark which room each fragile box belongs to.
